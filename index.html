Cover Letter

Dear Hiring Team,

I am excited to apply for the Social Media Lead position at Circle. With experience across digital marketing, social media, content development, SEO, paid advertising and audience engagement, combined with a Master’s degree in Global Marketing and Communications, I am excited by the opportunity to build and grow a social media function for a company operating at the intersection of technology, creators and community.

Throughout my marketing experience, I have developed content and digital strategies designed to attract, engage and convert audiences across multiple channels. My experience with social media, content creation, SEO, Google Ads, analytics and digital campaign execution has given me a strong understanding of how creative content and performance data can work together to drive meaningful results.

What particularly interests me about Circle is the opportunity to build a social-first content engine rather than simply manage existing social channels. I enjoy developing content ideas, identifying audience interests, creating content calendars and establishing repeatable workflows that make content production consistent and scalable. I am comfortable adapting longer-form ideas into shorter, engaging formats and using tools such as Canva, Pictory, Vyond and Powtoon to support visual and video content production.

I also bring an analytical approach to social media. My digital marketing experience has involved using performance data to understand audience behaviour, evaluate campaigns and identify opportunities for improvement. I would bring this same approach to Circle by paying close attention to metrics such as non-follower reach, watch-through, saves, shares and content cadence, while using those insights to continuously refine the content strategy.

My experience working across marketing and technology-focused environments has also strengthened my ability to collaborate with different stakeholders and translate ideas into practical content. I am comfortable working independently, managing competing priorities and creating organised content workflows while maintaining a strong focus on quality and brand consistency.

Circle’s focus on creators, communities and digital businesses strongly aligns with my interest in building meaningful digital experiences and content that people genuinely want to engage with. I would welcome the opportunity to bring my creativity, strategic thinking, digital marketing experience and hands-on approach to Circle’s growing social presence.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be delighted to discuss how my experience and approach could contribute to Circle’s social media and creator-growth ambitions.

Kind regards,
Stella Atuchukwu
